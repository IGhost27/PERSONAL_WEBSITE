# Product Brief

## Purpose
Ian Ortega's personal site should introduce the person before the resume: an engineer, builder, leader, and explorer whose work spans aerospace, healthcare technology, AI research, humanitarian infrastructure, and FitOut.

## Audience
- Recruiters and engineering leaders arriving from LinkedIn or a resume
- Researchers and collaborators evaluating Ian's technical work
- Viewers discovering Ian through FitOut or a QR code

## Core Routes
- Home: who Ian is
- Resume: what Ian has done
- FitOut: how Ian explores
- Projects: what Ian has built
- Contact: what comes next

## Source Of Truth
Use only details already verified in the repository, Ian's public LinkedIn profile, published research, and the FitOut channel. Missing photography remains an explicit replacement slot. Never invent project outcomes, travel, employment accomplishments, or social links.

## Design Direction
Modern editorial documentary with an outdoor sensibility: warm paper, charcoal, stone, forest green, and one expedition-orange accent. Large direct typography, thin route and timeline rules, monospace metadata, cinematic media proportions, fast restrained motion, and no decorative card grids.

## Content Principles
- Home makes Ian interesting without acting as a resume.
- Resume makes Ian credible through a web-native timeline.
- FitOut is immersive and image-led.
- Projects explain questions, process, and learning.
- Contact makes connection effortless.

## Technical Constraints
- React and Vite with HashRouter for GitHub Pages compatibility
- Accessible navigation, focus states, and reduced-motion support
- Responsive at 375px, 430px, 768px, and desktop
- Data-driven currently, resume, project, and adventure content
